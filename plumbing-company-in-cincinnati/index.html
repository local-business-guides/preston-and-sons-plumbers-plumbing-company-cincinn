Scope, visit timing and useful terms

A plumbing visit generally starts with a description of the concern and an assessment. The plumber can then explain the likely work and any factors that may affect timing or cost. Ask for the proposed scope in plain language, especially if the work could change after the initial assessment.

A few terms you may hear:
- Fixture: A plumbing-connected item, such as a sink or toilet.
- Shutoff valve: A valve used to stop water flow to a fixture or area.
- Estimate: An expected cost for the described work. Ask what it covers and what could change it.

The time needed can’t be confirmed without knowing the issue. The location of the problem, access and whether parts are needed can all affect how long the work takes. Ask the provider for a timing update based on the details you share.
